## Decisions Made
- **cmd.get_names type:** Used `'public_objects'` not `'all_objects'` — the latter raises `CmdException("unknown type: 'all_objects'")` in PyMOL 2.5.0. The research (sec 1.5) cited `'public_objects'` as the bioCHEMeleon-verified type; `'all_objects'` was a research-sec-4.2 inference that turned out invalid. Adjusted both bridge and smoke consistently.
- **cmd.get_extent return format:** PyMOL 2.5.0 returns `[[min_x, min_y, min_z], [max_x, max_y, max_z]]` (list of two 3-element lists), NOT the flat 6-element tuple `(min_x, min_y, min_z, max_x, max_y, max_z, ...)` the research assumed. Fixed place_head to unpack the nested structure.
- **frame_scene in box-only paths:** materialize calls frame_scene() in all paths after box load (including when no head is selected), so the user sees the box even without a head (SC2: "see a clearly visible boundary box").

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Fixed cmd.get_extent return format mismatch**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (smoke execution — head_load and materialize steps failed with IndexError)
- **Issue:** The research (sec 1.7) stated cmd.get_extent returns `(min_x, min_y, min_z, max_x, max_y, max_z, ...)` — a flat tuple. PyMOL 2.5.0 actually returns `[[min_x, min_y, min_z], [max_x, max_y, max_z]]` — a list of two 3-element lists. place_head accessed ext[0]+ext[3] (flat indexing), raising IndexError.
- **Fix:** Changed place_head to unpack `(min_x, min_y, min_z), (max_x, max_y, max_z) = ext` and compute centroid from the nested structure.
- **Files modified:** serpentrum/pymol_bridge.py
- **Verification:** Smoke head_load and materialize steps pass; place_head centers the head at origin.
- **Committed in:** 5a35e76 (Task 2 commit — fix discovered during smoke run)

**2. [Rule 3 - Blocking] Substituted cmd.get_names('all_objects') -> ('public_objects')**
- **Found during:** Task 2 (smoke execution — all 6 steps failed with CmdException)
- **Issue:** `cmd.get_names('all_objects')` raises `CmdException("unknown type: 'all_objects'")` in PyMOL 2.5.0. The research (sec 1.5) cited `'public_objects'` as the bioCHEMeleon-verified type; sec 4.2 used `'all_objects'` as an inference.
- **Fix:** Changed to `cmd.get_names('public_objects')` in both the bridge (cleanup_srp) and the smoke (all step functions). The research explicitly sanctioned this fallback: "'public_objects' is the fallback if 'all_objects' misbehaves — verify-at-execution".
- **Files modified:** serpentrum/pymol_bridge.py, smoke/03_viewer_bridge_smoke.py
