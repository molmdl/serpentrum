<objective>
Create the PURE pickup-spawn policy `serpentrum/spawn.py` (G3) — the one engine-adjacent behavior that exists nowhere today. PINNED POLICY (stated explicitly per the planning mandate):

- **One NEW spawn per resolution** (never batch-spawn). After begin_game spawns the first pickup, every capture resolution ('stacked' handled — success OR refusal) may spawn the next molecule, gated by a live-pickup ceiling (MAX_LIVE_PICKUPS = 4) so refusal-lingering pickups cannot accumulate unbounded while the game keeps progressing.
- **Cyclic molecule order:** records in anchored list order, wrapping forever (set_a has 5 species; cap default 10 requires species reuse — chemically valid, probe-verified 6-segment same-molecule chains are clash-safe).
- **Position:** LOOKAHEAD_A = 8.0 along the current heading + seeded lateral offset in [-6.0, +6.0] (0.1-quantized), validated against: wall margin 3.5 Å, head-centroid clearance 5.0 Å, chain-ATOM clearance 3.0 Å (keeps the engine's 2.5 Å sweep pickup-leg pre-check satisfiable near fresh spawns), live-pickup-centroid clearance 6.0 Å. Up to 32 seeded retries, then a deterministic grid-scan fallback, else None.
- **Seed:** zlib.crc32 over a canonical setup string (NEVER hash() — PYTHONHASHSEED randomizes str hashes across processes).
- **Uploads spawn and are skipped at capture** (honest STACK-03 pedagogy; locked decision 7).

Purpose: the engine has a capture leg but no spawn policy; gui_game needs a deterministic, WSL-testable spawner to seed `GameEngine(pickups=...)` and materialize viewer objects.
