## Decisions Made

- s9 drives the spawner with the head pinned at `(0.0, 0.0)` for every call.
  The 5.3 policy is position-neutral, so any box-wide spread must originate
  in the uniform draws; a moving scripted head could smuggle spread in
  without testing randomization.
- Spread floors (>= 3 quadrants, max head-distance > 30 A) are derived
  thresholds, not hardcoded coordinates — the retired lookahead bubble
  capped reach at ~10 A, so 30 A is a 3x-reach floor that cannot pass under
  the old policy.
- Live centroids accumulate WITHOUT applying `can_spawn`: MAX_LIVE is
  caller-side; the spawner itself never gates on it, so s9's maximal-live
  state exercises all clearance legs per step.
