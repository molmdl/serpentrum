<objective>
Swap the head-anchored lookahead+lateral candidate generator in `PickupSpawner._spawn` for seeded uniform sampling over the shrunk box, with a box-proportional minimum head distance so no unlucky draw lands "too simple" close to the snake.

Purpose: the entire Phase 5.3 behavior — owner feedback 2026-09-26 ("the new mol to be eaten spawn too close to the current position, so its likely too simple. make it more random in the box"). Today every candidate sits on a 12 A line segment 8.0 A ahead of the nose (LOOKAHEAD_A=8.0 + LATERAL_MAX_A=±6.0), so all pickups spawn within a 10 A bubble of the head (~1.3–1.7 s at 6.0 A/s) and the owner's box presets are never actually used. Surgical scope: `__init__`/`first`/`next_after` signatures stay IDENTICAL (heading becomes position-neutral, still validated) so `gui_game.py`/`game_engine.py`/`hud_logic.py` need zero edits — which also sidesteps the ROADMAP-flagged Phase-6 shared-file ordering hazard.

Output: randomized-but-seeded spawn policy in `serpentrum/spawn.py` + rewritten/extended pins in `tests/test_spawn.py`. TDD: RED -> GREEN -> (REFACTOR), 2-3 atomic commits.
</objective>
