Mitigation to propose at planning: order the viewer write FIRST and commit engine state only after a successful load (failure degrades exactly like the 32-draw None path — "benign: keep the previous wall[s]"). Alternatively wrap the wall tail in its own narrow try/except that never rejects the (already counted) capture. Needs a pin in the 9.1 gui tests either way.
