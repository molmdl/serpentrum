1. Open the plugin (Plugins → serpentrum). On the Setup tab click Start.
   EXPECT: dialog switches to the Game tab; countdown "3" "2" "1" "GO!" at ~1 s intervals in the big label AND the info box. [GAME-01]
2. During the countdown, watch the viewer.
   EXPECT: the head does NOT move until GO!. [GAME-01]
3. After GO!, watch for ~30 s without touching anything.
   EXPECT: the head moves forward continuously without stopping, at a CONSTANT pace (no speed-up/slow-down; ~0.3 Å per 0.1 s — reaches a box wall on the medium preset in roughly 2 minutes). [GAME-01, GAME-08]
4. Click the 3D viewer, then steer: press up, down, left, right mid-run.
   EXPECT: all four turn the snake (turn happens at the next tick; 180-degree reversals are refused — try pressing the reverse key, nothing should happen). [GAME-03]
5. Try to move the camera during play: left-drag, middle-drag, right-drag, wheel, Shift+left, Ctrl+left.
   EXPECT: the camera does NOT move at all; the view is flat (ortho); the orange boundary box stays fully visible for the whole run. [GAME-02]
6. Read the HUD during play.
   EXPECT: Elapsed counts up from 0:00 (matches a wall-clock stopwatch within ±1 s over ~30 s); "Remaining: 10" (or the configured cap) and stays there; the info box logs the countdown, "Move with the arrow keys.", and any "turn refused" lines. [GAME-07]
7. Click Pause mid-run.
   EXPECT: head freezes instantly; button label flips to "Resume"; elapsed label freezes. Click Resume.
   EXPECT: motion resumes; the elapsed timer does NOT jump by the paused duration. [GAME-07]
8. Click Restart mid-run.
   EXPECT: countdown re-runs 3-2-1; head returns to the box center; elapsed resets to 0:00; exactly ONE countdown runs (start it, then Restart again during the "3" — still only one chain; no double timers). [GAME-07, epoch guard]
9. Let the head run into a wall (don't steer).
   EXPECT: info box logs "crashed into boundary"; motion stops; the run is over (pause disabled). [GAME-01 end-of-run behavior]
10. After the crash, check for leaks: left-drag the viewer (rotates again), wheel (zooms), press arrows (no steering, no frame-step hijack), ortho look (perspective restored if it was on before).
   EXPECT: everything behaves as before the game. [teardown — Pitfall 9]
11. Start a new game, then close the serpentrum dialog mid-run (X button).
   EXPECT: no further movement in the viewer; mouse/keys behave normally. Reopen the plugin. [close-path teardown]
